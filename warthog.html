About Warthogs

  • Common Name: Warthog
  • Endangerment Status: Not ednangered and are classified as least concern
  • Phylum: Vertebrates
  • Class: Mammals
  • Subclass: Theria
  • Order: Artiodactyla
  • Family: Suinae
  • Genus:Phacochoerus
  • Size: 25-33in tall
  • markings: Warts, and slight hair over their body
  • Habitat: Sub-Saharan Africa
  • Diet: grasses, roots, and plants, supplemented by small animals and carrion
  • Behaviors: They spend their days foraging, resting in burrows, and fleeing from predators
  • Comunication: using a mix of vocal sounds, body language, and scent marking
  • Life Span: 10 to 15 years in the wild and 18 to 20 years in captivity
  • Predators: lions, leopards, cheetahs, spotted hyenas, African wild dogs, and Nile crocodiles

Warthogs have prominent warts on their faces as their name suggests, the warts on their faces are cartilaginous and protect their faces and eyes during fights and can gow up to 6 in in length in males they also have large upper tusks that are 10-24 in long in males and 6-10 in on the females the males use their tusks for fighting and defending themselfs, the female warthogs use them for defence.
