About The Gaboon Viper

  • Common Name: Gaboon Viper
  • Endangerment Status: Vulnerable
  • Phylum: Chordata
  • Class: Reptilia
  • Subclass: Viperidae
  • Order: Squamata
  • Family: Viperidae
  • Genus: Bitis
  • Size: average length of 4 to 6 feet
  • markings: pale, rectangular blotches down the back, dark yellow-edged hourglass shapes, and fawn or brown rhomboidal shapes on their flanks
  • Habitat: the rainforests and savannas of sub-Saharan Africa, primarily across West, Central, and parts of East and Southern Africa
  • Diet: small to medium-sized mammals, ground-dwelling birds, and occasionally amphibians
  • Behaviors: calm, nocturnal ambush predators
  • Comunication: chemical signals and physical behaviors
  • Life Span: 15 to 20 years
  • Predators: they have virtually no natural predators

The gaboon viper is on the bigger side of venomous snakes being the largest viper in africa its colloration and broad head mimics the shape and color of fallen leaves its camouflage is top teir though even being venomus this snake has a calm nature and rarely bites humans.
